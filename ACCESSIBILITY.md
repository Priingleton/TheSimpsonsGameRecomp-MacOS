# Accessibility

The aim is to make this port easier to play and its launcher easier to use. Accessibility
improvements and reports are welcome, including small fixes to controls, text, and navigation.

## Current options

This fork runs on **macOS Apple Silicon**. It supports controllers and keyboard and mouse input,
with rebindable controls. See [Configuration](README.md#configuration) for available settings.

## Known limits

- In-game prompts still show controller buttons when playing with a keyboard and mouse.
- Screen-reader support and full keyboard navigation in the Electron launcher have not been
  verified.
- The game retains much of the original interface. Text scaling, contrast, and colour
  accessibility have not been fully assessed.

## Reporting a barrier

Use this repository’s accessibility report issue template. Describe the task you were trying to
complete, where you got stuck, your macOS version, and any input device or assistive software
involved. A description is enough; screenshots and videos are optional. You do not need to share
a diagnosis or medical information.
